# FormalPantheon

FormalPantheon collects our formalization projects across different areas of mathematics.

## Projects

- [BoundedGaps](BoundedGaps): A Lean formalization of bounded gaps between primes, based on James Maynard's *Small Gaps Between Primes* and the breakthrough of Yitang Zhang's *Bounded Gaps Between Primes*, with the Bombieri--Vinogradov (BV) theorem as a central intermediate theorem.
- [RestrictedDigits](RestrictedDigits): A Lean formalization of James Maynard's theorem on primes with restricted decimal digits, proving uniform two-sided estimates for primes whose decimal expansion omits any prescribed digit, together with the infinitude corollary.
- [PeriodThree](PeriodThree): A Lean formalization of Li and Yorke's Theorem I and its period-three-implies-chaos corollary, from T.-Y. Li and J. A. Yorke's *Period Three Implies Chaos* (American Mathematical Monthly 82, 1975).
- [Waring](Waring): A Lean 4 formalization of Chen Jingrun's theorem that every positive integer is a sum of at most 37 fifth powers of nonnegative integers, and that 37 is the smallest such uniform bound, based on Chen's *Waring's Problem for g(5)=37* (Scientia Sinica 13, 1964).
